# Creative Direction and AI Disclosure

**Creator: Dustin (dustindwayne).** The original game concept, art-style direction, world, gameplay vision, and overall creative direction are his ideas. He directed the build and supplied its music and visual references.

**This game was built with AI assistance.** Codex/ChatGPT assisted with programming, implementation, generated visual assets, level-generation systems, integration, and testing. AI was used to realize and expand the creator's direction; this is not presented as entirely hand-coded or hand-drawn work.

The enemy and boss atlas includes AI-generated artwork. The retro pixel style and creative direction were specified by the creator. Music was supplied by the creator; no claim is made here about how those recordings were produced.

The VT323 font is distributed under the SIL Open Font License included with the project.

The refreshed GPU/city logo is AI-generated artwork based on the creator’s game and art direction. See the repository’s logo generation record.

Looper is based on a character sheet supplied by the creator, adapted with AI assistance to the game’s pixel style. Refreshed survivor, enemy and equipment atlases also use AI-assisted artwork. Source sheets and prompt records are retained.
