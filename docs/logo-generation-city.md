## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit/reinvent this game logo for Artificial Negligence, retaining the exact title and recognizable cream pixel lettering, dark navy background, cyan outlines and warm gold accents. The reference is the existing brand, not a layout to copy literally. Produce one finished professional square game logo, 1024x1024 or larger, generous safe margins. Represent the actual game: an underdog street survivor collecting GPU cores, reclaiming public AI, and reconstructing a city as graphics improve. Upper half: a bold central GPU microchip with glowing cyan core, framed by a small rooftop city silhouette and a single hooded survivor silhouette standing on a platform; no portrait detail. Left edge starts with chunky coarse square pixels; they become crisp, refined pixel-art circuitry and city shapes toward the right, visibly suggesting graphics evolution. Keep this a compact iconic emblem, no ships, no rockets, no photorealism, no complicated poster scene. Lower half: large highly legible two-line exact title ARTIFICIAL / NEGLIGENCE. Spell both words correctly. Thick cream block-pixel letters, crisp cyan keyline, subtle gold corners. Strong negative space, restrained neon glow, graphic-design polish, clean alignment. No slogan, no extra text, no watermark. Opaque near-black navy background; pixel art language throughout.
