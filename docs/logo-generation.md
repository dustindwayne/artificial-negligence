Reference2 is Looper identity: curly brown hair, black rectangular glasses, warm skin, black patched street jacket with vivid blue trim, gray shirt, fingerless gloves, white/blue shoes and blue-core backpack. Upper emblem must prominently feature ONE recognizable Looper, in a confident energetic running/leaping platformer pose, holding a gold GPU core, full body completely visible. His face, glasses and curly hair should read clearly, matching the supplied pixel-art character, not a hooded silhouette. Compose the character with a compact cyan GPU chip and restrained rooftop/circuit motif; pixel fragments suggest graphics rebuilding. Prioritize Looper and title, clean iconic composition with generous dark negative space. No ships, no extra characters, no photorealism, no slogan or extra text. Professional polished pixel-art game branding, 1:1 square, strong at thumbnail size. No clipping of title, hair, shoes, backpack, character or border; keep generous safe margins.
