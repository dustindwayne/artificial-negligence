# Artificial Negligence — Unity 2D / 2.4.11

See the root game README for downloads, current screenshots and features.

Six playable characters: Looper, Ai Wrath, Davey Goats, Brad Jammin’, Mikey O’Redd and Prison Mike. The Founder is a story NPC. Fresh unlocks: Brad after stage 6, Mikey after his stage 9 virtual fight, Prison Mike after stage 12, Wrath after his third encounter in stage 13, Davey after his stage 18 virtual fight. Prior unlocks are retained. Retired IDs remain reserved.

The three-act campaign covers 90 generated stages; story details are in STORY.md. Found equipment has independent item levels and fixed rarity. Excellent clears roll 5% Ultra, 10% Legend and 85% Rare once per stage. Items are manually equipped. No starting or XP-granted gear.

## Build and validate

Unity 6000.6.5f1. BuildRelease.All builds Windows, Linux, Android and WebGL after validating route reachability, puzzles, equipment and 124 isolated sprite frames. Runtime checks cover the three-act campaign, virtual-host unlocks, inventory, prologue/title, HUD and input routing. Signing credentials remain local.
