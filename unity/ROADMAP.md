# Art Roadmap: Artist-Led Characters and Animation

## Current status — 2.4.11

The current visuals remain AI-generated or AI-assisted under creator direction. Six characters, artwork dialogue, GPU-driven visual tiers, Windows/web controller input and a browser build now exist. Creator-drawn replacements, distinct ally powers and hardware controller/device QA remain planned.

## Direction

Move away from fully AI-generated visuals toward characters drawn by Dustin. AI will assist with individual animation frames while the creator controls the designs, poses, style, and final artwork.

## 1. Establish the hand-drawn foundation

- Draw the protagonist first, then creatures, bots, and bosses.
- Create reference sheets covering proportions, silhouettes, colors, equipment, and expressions.
- Set a consistent pixel scale and palette for in-game sprites.

## 2. Draw the key animation poses

- Author the important poses for idle, running, jumping, landing, attacks, damage, and defeat.
- Define timing, contact points, and the intended movement before filling in frames.
- Keep original drawings and working files as the source of truth.

## 3. Use AI as a frame assistant

- Supply the hand-drawn references and neighboring keyframes for each request.
- Use AI to propose in-between frames and small motion variations.
- Preserve character identity, proportions, equipment, palette, and pixel scale.
- Review and redraw each proposed frame where needed; include only creator-approved frames.

## 4. Replace visuals gradually

- Replace the protagonist's sprites before moving through regular enemies and bosses.
- Test every animation in the game for readability, timing, alignment, and combat feedback.
- Keep existing generated art as temporary placeholders until replacements are ready.
- Extend the same artist-led process to scenery, props, and UI artwork over time.

## 5. Keep attribution clear

- Credit original character designs and hand-drawn keyframes to their artist.
- Identify AI-assisted frames and any fully generated placeholders that remain.
- Update credits as assets are replaced; retain sketches and revision history.

**Goal:** A game whose visual identity comes from the creator's drawings, with AI supporting the animation process.

This is a future direction, not a claim that the current sprites are hand-drawn.
