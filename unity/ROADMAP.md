# Art Roadmap

## Current status — 2.5.0

Six playable characters, story cutscenes, GPU-driven visual tiers and Windows, Linux, Android and browser builds are available. Further character artwork, animation polish and hardware testing are planned.

## Direction

Develop creator-drawn character designs and key poses, then refine the supporting frames under creator review.

- Establish reference sheets for proportions, silhouettes, palettes, equipment and expressions.
- Draw key poses for idle, running, jumping, climbing, attacks, damage and defeat.
- Refine in-between frames while preserving identity and consistent pixel scale.
- Replace artwork gradually and test readability, timing, sprite alignment and combat feedback.
- Extend the process to enemies, scenery, props and interface artwork.

This describes planned artwork improvements, rather than the production method of current assets.
