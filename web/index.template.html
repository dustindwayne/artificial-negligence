01 / THE MISSION

The future belongs
to everyone.

The AI boom opened the world. Then reality fractured into pixels. The Public Signal blames masked enemies and promises to restore everything if you bring back the GPUs.

You are Looper. Cross the city, recover the cores, find allies and question the signal. A clearer picture is not always the truth.

RUNNER'S NOTE / READ FIRST

Nothing is handed to you.

Find your weapons. Salvage your armor. Earn your allies.

Every distinct GPU restores the world's graphics. Build momentum through combat and close calls to earn temporary AI help.
